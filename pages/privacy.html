Privacy Policy

InsureDrive Financial Technologies Ltd. is committed to protecting your vehicle data, policy information, and privacy.

Last Updated: September 10, 2026 • IRDAI Web Aggregator Compliance

1. Overview & Commitment

InsureDrive Financial Technologies Ltd. ("InsureDrive", "we", "our", or "us") operates as a licensed digital insurance web aggregator. We respect your personal privacy and are committed to safeguarding all information collected through our website, mobile interface, and policy comparison tools. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, process, store, and protect your information.

2. Information We Collect

To generate accurate car insurance quotes and facilitate seamless digital policy renewals, we collect the following categories of information:

  • Vehicle Registration Details: Car registration number, RTO location, vehicle make, model, variant, registration year, and IDV valuation metrics.
  • Policy History Data: Previous insurance policy expiry date, existing insurer name, claim history, and No Claim Bonus (NCB) percentage.
  • Contact Details: Owner name, mobile phone number, email address, and pincode required for e-policy delivery and regulatory invoices.
  • Technical Diagnostics: IP address, device type, browser specifications, and session identifiers collected via secure cookies for platform performance.

3. Zero Spam Telemarketing Guarantee

🛡️ Strict No-Telemarketing Policy

Unlike traditional insurance portals, InsureDrive strictly prohibits selling, leasing, or renting customer contact details to third-party telemarketing agencies or call centers. You will never receive unwanted promotional spam calls.

4. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used strictly for legitimate insurance transaction purposes:

  1. Fetching verified RTO vehicle specifications via official government VAHAN integration API gateways.
  2. Generating transparent side-by-side premium quotes from IRDAI-authorized general insurance partners.
  3. Processing official digital e-policies, tax invoices, and cashless claim registration requests.
  4. Sending automated renewal notifications 30 days prior to policy expiry to protect your No Claim Bonus (NCB).

5. Data Encryption & Security Standards

All data transmissions across InsureDrive are encrypted using 256-bit Transport Layer Security (TLS/SSL). Customer policy data is stored on ISO/IEC 27001 certified cloud infrastructure with strict multi-factor access controls and automated threat prevention.
